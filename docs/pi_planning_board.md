# Program Increment (PI) Planning Board: AiDLC vs SDLC Knowledge, Security & Decision Portal
**Agile Release Train (ART):** Enterprise Architecture, AI Transformation & CyberSec ART  
**PI Objective:** Deliver an authoritative, interactive web portal evaluating Artificial Intelligence Development Life Cycle (AiDLC) versus traditional Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), providing deep comparative analysis, 5-layer guardrails architecture, enterprise governance (ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act), AI cybersecurity threat vectors (OWASP LLM Top 10), and real-world enterprise case studies.

---

## 1. Feature Epics & User Stories (Definition of Ready - DoR Verified)

### Epic 1: Architectural Comparison & Paradigm Shift Matrix
- **User Story US-101**: Side-by-Side Paradigm Breakdown
  - **Given**: An architect or engineering lead visits the portal
  - **When**: They access the Paradigm Overview section
  - **Then**: They must see a comprehensive comparison across 12 distinct dimensions (Determinism, Imperative logic vs Emergent weights, Boolean assertions vs Evals, Cybersecurity threat vectors) with instant search.

- **User Story US-102**: Phase-by-Phase Comparative Lifecycle Journey
  - **Given**: A development team planning an AI transition
  - **When**: They navigate through the 7 standard development phases
  - **Then**: The system displays side-by-side workflows for SDLC vs AiDLC with explicit Security & Compliance Gates for each phase.

### Epic 2: Advantages, Trade-offs & TCO Analysis
- **User Story US-201**: Dual-Perspective Advantage & Trade-off Matrix
  - **Given**: A technology executive or project sponsor
  - **When**: They review the pros and cons section
  - **Then**: They can examine rigorous trade-offs covering compute costs (CPU vs GPU/tokens), explainability, regulatory compliance, and long-term maintenance overhead.

### Epic 3: Interactive Decision Framework ("What to Use & When")
- **User Story US-301**: Interactive Fit Assessment Quiz
  - **Given**: A team unsure whether to build using SDLC, AiDLC, or Hybrid Compound AI
  - **When**: They answer 5 strategic questions (Problem domain, Explainability, Data maturity, Error tolerance, Budget)
  - **Then**: The portal delivers an algorithmic verdict with confidence score, radar profile, and guardrail checklist.

### Epic 4: The Compound AI & Dual-Loop Architecture
- **User Story US-401**: Deterministic Sandwich Architectural Blueprint
  - **Given**: Full-stack engineers building modern enterprise software
  - **When**: They review the Hybrid / AI-Augmented SDLC section
  - **Then**: They are presented with architectural blueprints showing how outer deterministic SDLC gates protect inner probabilistic AiDLC loops.

### Epic 5: Multi-Layer Guardrails & Security Defense (NEW)
- **User Story US-501**: 5-Layer Enterprise AI Guardrails Taxonomy
  - **Given**: DevSecOps and AI Architects
  - **When**: They open the Guardrails Architecture section
  - **Then**: They can inspect Layer 1 (Input/Prompt), Layer 2 (Retrieval/RAG), Layer 3 (Model/Inference), Layer 4 (Output/Assertions), and Layer 5 (Operational/Cost) with active mechanisms and industry toolchains.

- **User Story US-502**: Cybersecurity Threat Defense (OWASP LLM Top 10)
  - **Given**: Cybersecurity and AppSec teams
  - **When**: They review the Cybersecurity section
  - **Then**: They see a direct comparison of traditional AppSec vs AI Sec and the full OWASP Top 10 for LLMs with severity ratings and mitigation protocols.

### Epic 6: Enterprise AI Governance & Case Studies
- **User Story US-601**: Global Regulatory & Governance Standards
  - **Given**: Compliance and enterprise risk officers
  - **When**: They inspect the Governance tab
  - **Then**: They view full comparative mappings for ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, and the EU AI Act.

- **User Story US-602**: 12 Cross-Domain Real-World Enterprise Case Studies
  - **Given**: Domain architects from any major industry
  - **When**: They filter case studies
  - **Then**: They can examine 12 case studies covering Fintech, Healthcare, Aerospace, Automotive, Telecom, Insurance, Logistics, Energy, Legal, HR, and Public Sector.

### Epic 7: Enterprise FinOps TCO & Latency Simulation (NEW)
- **User Story US-701**: Real-Time FinOps TCO & Token Latency Simulator
  - **Given**: A CTO, VP of Engineering, or Cloud FinOps Lead
  - **When**: They adjust daily query volume, token length, cache hit rates, and model tiers
  - **Then**: The system computes real-time comparative monthly/annual costs, P95 latencies, unit economics per 1,000 queries, and carbon emissions across Pure SDLC, Pure AiDLC, Compound AI, and Private GPU Clusters.

### Epic 8: CAB Production Readiness Gate & Code Starter Toolkit (NEW)
- **User Story US-801**: Change Advisory Board (CAB) Go/No-Go Audit Gate
  - **Given**: A Technical Lead or CAB committee preparing a production release
  - **When**: They evaluate the 15 enterprise criteria across 5 governance categories
  - **Then**: The system computes a weighted CAB Readiness Score (%) and exports an official signed-off audit certificate in Markdown.

- **User Story US-802**: Production Code Starter Toolkit & Cheat Sheets
  - **Given**: Fullstack developers and AI engineers
  - **When**: They access the Code Toolkit
  - **Then**: They can copy vetted production implementations for the Deterministic Sandwich Pattern (TypeScript/Node.js and Python/FastAPI), GitHub Actions CI/CD Eval Gates, and declarative Guardrails JSON configurations.

- **User Story US-803**: Universal Spotlight Quick Search (Ctrl+K) & URL Deep-Linking
  - **Given**: Any portal visitor
  - **When**: They press `Ctrl+K` or type in the search bar
  - **Then**: They can instantly search across all 12 dimensions, 7 visual diagrams, 12 case studies, guardrails, governance frameworks, and code templates with instant jump and URL hash synchronization.
  - **Given**: Risk Officers and Enterprise Compliance Auditors
  - **When**: They view the Governance section
  - **Then**: They can review ISO/IEC 42001 (AIMS), NIST AI RMF 1.0, and EU AI Act (Risk Tiers) alongside operational mechanisms (AI-CAB, Model Cards, Golden Sets).

- **User Story US-602**: Domain Scenarios & Real-World Case Studies
  - **Given**: Practitioners seeking precedent
  - **When**: They browse Case Studies
  - **Then**: They can examine detailed architectural dissections from Global Banking, Clinical Healthcare, Aerospace Defense, and Tier-1 E-Commerce.
